# 🚲 Cyclist-Bike-Share-Analysis-Project


## 📌 Objective

Analyze 12 months of Cyclistic bike-share trip data to uncover behavioral trends among **casual riders** and **annual members**, with the goal of recommending strategies to convert casual users into loyal subscribers.

---

## 🧠 Business Task

> How do annual members and casual riders use Cyclistic bikes differently?

Cyclistic’s marketing team wants to understand usage patterns to create targeted membership campaigns that encourage casual riders to become annual members.

---

## 🛠 Tools & Technologies

- **Data Source:** Divvy/Cyclistic Public Trip Data (12 months)
- **Data Cleaning:** Excel Power Query
- **Analysis & Visualization:** Excel Pivot Tables, Tableau

---

## 🧹 Data Preparation Steps

1. Downloaded and extracted 12 months of trip data.
2. Converted all `.csv` files to `.xls` format.
3. Combined the data using **Power Query** in Excel.
4. Performed data cleaning:
   - Removed nulls, duplicates, and invalid durations
   - Added `ride_length` and `day_of_week` fields
5. Built Pivot Tables and Tableau visuals for analysis.

---

## 📊 Key Visualizations & Insights

### 1. Average Ride Duration
- Casual riders take significantly longer rides (20–25 mins) than members (10–15 mins).
- Casual usage is more recreational, while members are likely commuting.

### 2. Ride Volume by Weekday
- Casual riders peak on **weekends**; members ride more on **weekdays**.
- Indicates leisure vs. routine/work-related use.

### 3. Bike Type Preferences
- **Electric bikes** are the most used overall, especially among casual riders.
- Members show a more balanced use of classic and electric bikes.

---

## ✅ Recommendations

1. **Promote Weekend Membership Bundles**  
   Target casual riders who ride on Saturdays and Sundays.

2. **Highlight Electric Bike Access in Plans**  
   Create and advertise membership tiers that include electric bike access.

3. **Trigger In-App Messaging**  
   Suggest memberships to casual riders after multiple long or electric rides.
